Intranet

An intranet is a private network accessible only to an organization's members, employees, or authorized users. It is used to share information, resources, and communication within the organization.

Common Intranet Uses

  • Internal company portals and dashboards
  • Employee directories and HR resources
  • Document management and file sharing
  • Internal wikis and knowledge bases
  • Project management tools
  • Company news and announcements
  • Training materials and onboarding

Intranets can save companies millions by reducing paper usage, improving communication speed, and centralizing information. Many use internal versions of Web 2.0 tools like wikis and social networks.
