Extranet

An extranet is a private network that allows controlled access to external users, such as partners, suppliers, or customers. It extends an intranet beyond the organization.

Extranet Use Cases

  • Supplier portals for inventory and order management
  • Customer portals for support and account management
  • Partner collaboration platforms
  • Secure file sharing with external stakeholders
  • Project management with clients or contractors

Extranets often use VPNs, secure web portals, or cloud-based platforms to provide access while maintaining security. They can improve communication and efficiency between organizations.
